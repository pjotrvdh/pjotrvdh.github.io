Pong, but with a twist. Your see-through paddle is at the front; the opponent guards the far end.

  • Move with the mouse, by dragging on a touch screen, or with the arrow keys or WASD.
  • Before every serve the game counts down from 3, then the ball serves itself. Move as it leaves to curve your serve.
  • Curve the ball: move your paddle as you hit it. The faster you move, the more it bends.
  • Hit the ball in the centre of your paddle for a bonus. Clear a level fast for the level bonus.
  • Take the opponent's 3 lives to clear a level. You have 5 lives for all ten levels.
  • Play online: make a room and send the code or link to a friend, or join theirs. Both of you score; when one runs out of lives, the higher score wins.
