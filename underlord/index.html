Dungeon Heart

Cheats

The heroes of the surface world are coming for your Dungeon Heart. Dig into the dark, build rooms, attract creatures and survive six invasions.

  • Click a block to mark it for digging, or drag a box over blocks. Your imps dig, haul gold and claim new ground.
  • The map is hidden in clouds, but gold seams and portals shine through. Mark blocks anywhere, even in the clouds, and dig to find treasure and wandering creatures.
  • A portal near your Dungeon Heart is always visible. Claim it to attract creatures. Each creature needs a free bed in your Lair; stronger creatures also need bigger rooms. Each map has a limited pool of creatures.
  • Invading heroes show as white dots until they reach ground you have discovered.
  • Click a creature to pick it up and right-click your territory to drop it where the fighting is. With an empty hand, right-click a creature to slap it: slapped imps work faster.
  • Scroll the map by holding the right mouse button and moving, with the arrow keys or WASD, or by moving the pointer to the edge of the view. Ctrl + arrows (or Q and E) turn and zoom the view, as do the mouse wheel and pinching. Right-click puts a room or spell away.
